Sample photos for the niche demos, shown in the photo frames through data-src (see shared/photoslots.js).
Small WebP files, cropped to the size of their frame. Each page loads only its own photos, lazily.
Sources: AI-generated images Kfir sent (two collages, then five single scenes for plumber, movers,
electrician, trainer and tutor). Business names and fake lettering on shirts and boxes were inpainted,
and yellow gym balls recoloured. The trainer cover is mirrored so the magazine headline clears her face.
