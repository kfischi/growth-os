# CLAUDE.md

## What this repo is

Two things live here:

1. **Growth OS** (`src/`, `supabase/`): a multi-tenant Next.js app. One deployment serves many business sites, keyed on domain, with AI-proposed copy that the owner approves. See `README.md`.
2. **Kfir's side-income channel** (`demos/`, `clients/`, `docs/side-income/`): lead systems for small local businesses. A landing page, an instant WhatsApp reply, follow-up reminders and a monthly report, sold as a setup fee plus a monthly retainer.

Kfir owns and runs the side-income channel. It is **not** Multibrawn. Never put Multibrawn branding on its pages, and never mix it with other projects. The one exception is legal: the receipts are issued by Multi brawn (עוסק פטור), and the terms and the privacy page name it with its number and e-mail, by the owner's choice without the private phone or the address.

## House rules for anything a client or prospect sees

- **Hebrew:** correct, short and plain. No spelling mistakes. Address the reader in plural (`אתם`) unless you know who it is.
- **The brand speaks, not a person.** The site, the demos and the AI chat speak as Multi Landing, in plural ("אנחנו", "נחזור אליכם"). Never put Kfir's name, or a first-person "I" for the studio, on anything a visitor sees. (A fictional demo business may speak as its own owner.) Outreach messages Kfir sends himself are the exception.
- **No overpromising.** Never promise "double your clients". The promise is "no enquiry gets lost".
- **No yellow** in any palette. Aim for modern and premium, not cheap.
- **Mobile first and RTL.** Every page passes `node scripts/qa-page.cjs <dir> [page]` before it ships.
- **The client owns their assets.** The domain is registered in the client's name. Builder sites are hosted in כפיר's Netlify account; the site's files are in the repo and are handed over if the client leaves. Pages may say a site is built to the privacy law and the accessibility standard; never promise protection from fines.
- **Paid clients use the official WhatsApp Cloud API.** Unofficial gateways such as Evolution API are for demos and internal use only, because they risk getting the number banned.

Each trade sees the ones that suit it (`looksFor`, `catalog(tradeId)`): the technicians six (שרטוט, תעודת אחריות, שלט על הרכב, כרטיס עבודה, מוקד, פסיפס); beauty, care, events and lessons (cosmetics, nails, barber, hair, lashes and brows, hair removal, makeup, bridal, massage, physio, trainer, yoga, psychologist, social worker, photographer, DJ, catering, cakes, driving, tutor, music, dog trainer) five: סטודיו (an arch with the business's first letter, a serif), מחירון (a salon price list of the owner's own services and prices with dotted lines, never an invented price), כרטיס תור (an appointment card with hours and response time, never a free or booked slot, "בוחרים זמן שנוח לכם, ואנחנו מאשרים"), פסיפס and תעודת אחריות. The first of a trade's set is its default look. The owner picks one as the questionnaire's first question and sees the draft change; later through "עיצוב: תבנית וצבעים" under the chat, also in self edit. Stored in `content.look` and `content.palette`, sent to `/api/builder/answer` as `style`, no AI. The certificate's seal shows only the owner's own warranty or years.
- **Payment, the address and the automatic publish (Grow)** (`lib/grow.mjs`, `lib/billing.mjs`, `functions/grow-webhook.mjs`, `functions/billing.mjs`, `0017_billing.sql`): after approval the owner picks the address (up to 5 free `<slug>.<SITES_DOMAIN>` names, the business name transliterated by Claude, or their own; optionally their own domain) and pays on Grow's page: monthly (the setup today) or a year ahead (setup plus 10 months). The card is saved as a Grow token. `/api/grow-webhook` never takes the callback at its word: it asks Grow (`getPaymentProcessInfo`), checks the sum, settles once, calls `approveTransaction`, and publishes by itself (`publishDraft`, the same as כפיר's button): repo, hosting, the lead system, and the owner's domain added as waiting. A lost callback is caught by the return page (`pay-check`) and the 15-minute run (`runPending`), which also retries a failed publish 3 times. The builder then shows the domain's two DNS records ("כמה שעות, עד 48"), a "לבדוק עכשיו" button, the panel link once, the subscription, invoices and cancel (no notice: it runs to the end of what was paid). `functions/billing.mjs` charges the token daily at the billing day; a refusal retries every 3 days with "לעדכן כרטיס" in the builder, and 4 refusals pause the lead system (the page stays). "נתקעתם? כתבו לנו" on every step after approval alerts כפיר. The Grow account belongs to the partner business that issues the receipts (an עוסק פטור: Grow issues a receipt, קבלה, for every payment, never a tax invoice, and no VAT is added; pages say "קבלה", not "חשבונית"), not to כפיר. Without `GROW_PAGE_CODE` the old way stays: invoice on WhatsApp and כפיר marks it paid. Field names follow Grow's reference; try them with `scripts/grow-check.mjs` on the sandbox before going live. Tested in `scripts/tests/billing.test.mjs` and `billing-page.test.mjs`. The prices are also in `PRICES` in `lib/billing.mjs`.
- **Pilot** (guide: `docs/side-income/PILOT.md`): an invite link `build/?p=<code>` with a code from `PILOT_CODES` (comma separated, checked on the server) marks the draft `content.pilot`; the builder says it is a pilot with no payment, the approval has no payment step (`billingView.on` is false, `checkout` refuses, no `PAY_URL_*` links), כפיר is alerted "אתר פיילוט מחכה לפרסום", and `/build/admin/` tags it "פיילוט" and publishes it straight from `client_approved` (`isPilot` in `lib/builder.mjs`). No subscription is created. An unknown code is the normal builder.
- **Questionnaire** (`templates/plumber-guide.mjs`, `guideFor(trade)`, the template's `GUIDE`): one question per topic with answers to pick (regions and their towns, the trade's common services with sample price ranges the owner keeps or changes, response time, hours, years, warranty, ready headlines and intros), and a field for what isn't listed. Answers go through `/api/builder/answer` with the chat's own checks and no AI call; the chat stays for anything free-form.
- **"למדו את העסק שלי"**: under the builder chat the owner uploads up to 3 photos (a price list, a flyer, a card) or pastes text; `/api/builder/learn` (`learnTurn`, Claude vision, the chat's own schema and checks, 8 an hour) fills the fields from it and invents nothing. The photos aren't stored; the history keeps a line of text. The privacy page says the material goes to Anthropic.
- **Self edit** (`0004_self_edit.sql`): publishing turns on self edit (included in the monthly), and it turns off when the monthly stops (ended or suspended) and back on when a card update resumes it; כפיר can turn it off or on per site in `/build/admin/`. The owner returns to their private `/build/` link and changes the live site in the same chat; nothing goes live until they press "לפרסם את השינויים" (`/api/builder/publish`, one commit to `clients/<slug>/`). They can also discard changes or load the version before the last publish (`live` and `prev_live`). Monthly allowance: `BUILDER_EDIT_MESSAGES` (60) and `BUILDER_EDIT_PUBLISHES` (10). Works only for sites built from a builder template.
- "Save to contacts": a published site gets `contact.vcf` (`vcard()` in the template, served as `text/vcard` by `_headers`) and a link to it. In the panel every tagged link has a QR code to download (SVG or PNG), drawn in the browser with `shared/vendor/qrcode-generator-2.0.4.js` (MIT).
- Guides on client sites: the template shows the trade's urgent guides on the main page (the chat links there) and builds `guides.html` with all of them and FAQPage JSON-LD, from the same `shared/guides/<trade>.json`.
- **SEO at deploy** (`render(..., { url, credit })`): with the site's address the deploy adds a canonical link and og:url/og:image, the LocalBusiness url and image, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` (home and guides); the title leads with the trade and the area; privacy and accessibility are `noindex, follow`; a domain swap redeploys with the new address. The repo copy and drafts have none of it.
- **Service pages** (published sites only): one `service-<hash>.html` per service (`servicePage(name)`, stable for the same name), with the owner's facts (range, time, response time, hours, warranty), the towns, the trade guides that mention the service (`guidesFor`), the other services, Service and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, its own title and description, in the sitemap, linked from the price table; "לפתוח קריאה בצ׳אט" goes to `./?q=<service>` and the main page opens the chat with it picked. Never a page per town with the name swapped (Google treats those as doorway spam). A renamed service's old page leaves the repo at the next publish.
- **Area pages** (published sites only, `content.regions`, `REGIONS` and `regionsReady()` in the template): up to 5 `area-<hash>.html` pages (`areaPage(name)`), one per area the owner wrote about: their own words (60 to 500 characters, never the same words for two areas), the area's towns, its response time, the services and the other areas, with LocalBusiness `areaServed` and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, in the sitemap and linked from the main page's area section. An area without the owner's words gets no page. Filled by the questionnaire's optional "regions" step or the chat (`regions` in the output schema); the chats' facts include them. Tested in `scripts/tests/area-pages.test.mjs`.
- **Straight to the search engines** (`lib/search.mjs`): every deploy (`hostSite`) adds the site's IndexNow key file and sends its indexable pages to IndexNow (Bing and others; nothing to set up), and with `GSC_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` and `GSC_PROPERTY` (a Domain property for `SITES_DOMAIN`, the service account a full user) submits the sitemap to Google Search Console. The owner's panel then shows Google appearances and clicks for 28 days (`searchImpressions`). A client's own domain is their own property: IndexNow only. Tested in `scripts/tests/search.test.mjs`.
- **The launch kit** (`lib/launch.mjs`, in the builder once the site is live): what brings the first visitors before Google ranks a new site. The Google Business Profile steps from the owner's facts, a WhatsApp status and a post for local groups in the owner's voice, each with a copy button and, on a נציג AI site, its own tagged link (made once: "פרופיל בגוגל", "סטטוס בוואטסאפ", "קבוצות ופוסטים") so the panel shows what each brought. It promises nothing.
- **Social links** (`lib/social.mjs`, `content.social`): the owner's Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. `socialUrl()` turns what they type (a URL, `instagram.com/x`, `@x`, a bare `x` for Instagram/TikTok, a Facebook page name, a YouTube `@handle` or `/channel/…`) into one clean https profile URL on the network's own host, tracking query dropped, and refuses anything else; the chat's output schema (`social`), the questionnaire's optional "social" step and `learnTurn` all go through it in `applyUpdate`, and the template checks again before showing one. The site shows a row of single-colour icon links ("עקבו אחרינו") in the contact section and on every service and area page, and `sameAs` in the LocalBusiness JSON-LD. Plain links only: no embed, no pixel, so no cookies and no privacy change. The launch kit adds a link for each network's bio (the ones the owner gave, or all four): tagged on נציג AI ("ביו באינסטגרם", "פייסבוק", "טיקטוק", "יוטיוב"), the plain address on נוכחות. Tested in `scripts/tests/social.test.mjs`.
- **Fonts from the site itself** (`demos/shared/fonts/`, made by `node scripts/fetch-fonts.mjs` from each look's Google Fonts, Hebrew and Latin only, SIL OFL): a deployed site and a draft get the look's `@font-face` rules inline with the body font preloaded; the deploy copies the files to `assets/fonts/` (cached for a year). Nothing goes to Google, and the privacy page says so. Run the script again when a look's fonts change. The chat's scripts load with `defer` and the page's own script waits for `DOMContentLoaded`; only `a11y.js` stays in the head. Measured with Lighthouse 12 on a sample site (mobile, local server with brotli): performance 98, accessibility 100, best practices 96 (only because the test sandbox blocks the lead system's calls), SEO 100.
- **Credit line** at the bottom of every client page: "האתר נבנה ב-<BRAND_NAME> · אתרים שבונים עסקים", linking to `BRAND_URL` (default this site) with `utm_source=client&utm_campaign=<slug>`, the brand's name as the anchor. Env: `BRAND_NAME` (default "Multi Landing"), `BRAND_URL`.
- Schema.org: a published site gets LocalBusiness JSON-LD from the owner's facts (`templates/schema.mjs`: type by trade, phone, towns, hours when they parse, services with price ranges). Nothing invented: no address, no rating. The home page has its own ProfessionalService block.
- Photos (`PHOTOS` in the template): `portrait`, six work photos `work1`..`work6` with titles `work1_title`..`work6_title`, `before`, `after` and `hero`. The browser shrinks each (1600 pixels for `hero`, `before` and `after`, 1200 for the rest) and re-encodes it to WebP (JPEG on old Safari, which also drops EXIF); the server takes JPEG or WebP up to 1 MB and stores its width and height (`imageSize`), which the page writes on the `<img>`. On the site the work photos sit in rows of up to three (two and four in pairs, a short last row centred, two columns on a phone), all lazy-loaded. A draft marks only the first three empty frames; 4 to 6 show once filled.
- The top of the site, the owner's pick (`content.hero_media`, "תמונה בראש האתר" or "סרטון בראש האתר" in the builder's photo panel, saved through `/api/builder/answer` as `hero`, no AI): one of them, never both (`heroKind()` in the template; without a pick, the video if there is one). Only the picked one is published. A photo (slot `hero`) sits behind the top on a computer with the text side washed in the hero colour; on a phone it is a band in full above the text. With `GEMINI_API_KEY` in Netlify (optional `GEMINI_IMAGE_MODEL`, default `gemini-2.5-flash-image`) the builder offers "ליצור תמונת רקע": `/api/builder/hero-image` asks Google's image model for a 16:9 photo of the work's world (no people, faces, text, logos or yellow), and the page saves it like an upload. A few cents an image; limited per IP and per draft.
- Before and after (optional): photo slots `before` and `after` plus `ba_title`. With both photos the site gets a slider (self-contained in the template); a draft shows the frame from the first photo.
- Videos (optional): one at the top (`video`, `/api/builder/video`) and up to 5 short ones in a "סרטונים מהעבודה" section (`videos`, `/api/builder/clip`, `0019_videos.sql`, each with an optional title up to 34 characters). For each, the browser cuts 10 seconds and converts them to an H.264 MP4 with no sound (up to 720 pixels on the short side, 30 frames, 1.4 Mbit/s, about 1.8 MB; 4.4 MB at most, the server takes 4.5 MB) plus a WebP first frame as the poster, using Mediabunny (`demos/shared/vendor/`, loaded only when a video is picked); 12 videos per IP in 10 minutes. The top video takes the drawing's place and starts only after the page has loaded. The short videos load nothing with the page: their posters come when the section is a screen away, a file only when it plays (`preload="none"`, the source set then); one plays at a time, only on screen. None plays by itself for reduced motion, save-data, a slow network or `html.a11y-still`; each has a pause button. Measured with Lighthouse 12 (mobile) on the full-media sample (`full.html` from `builder-render.mjs`: a photo at the top, six work photos, before and after, five videos): 207 KB on load against 202 KB for the sample with three work photos. Tests run the same conversion in VP9, because the test browser has no H.264 encoder.

## Client sites

A paying client gets a real site in `clients/<slug>/`, copied from the trade demo and cleaned of every demo mark. The worked example is `clients/sample-plumber/` (a fictional business, kept `noindex`). Each site is self-contained (its own `assets/`), hosted on its own Netlify site. Builder sites are created and deployed automatically (see Hosting above). Hand-made sites go preferably in the client's own free account and are deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy-clients.yml` from `clients/<slug>/netlify.json` and a token in GitHub secrets. Guide: `docs/side-income/CLIENT_SITES.md`.

## Checks

- **Everything at once, before every PR:** `bash scripts/check-all.sh`. It runs qa-page on every demo and client page, the Hebrew lint, every demo chat to its WhatsApp handoff on phone and desktop (`scripts/tests/demo-chats.cjs`), the lead system end to end against fakes (`scripts/tests/lead-system.test.mjs`), the function bundles and lint.
- Hebrew copy: `node scripts/hebrew-copy-lint.cjs <file-or-dir>` flags AI-sounding and bureaucratic Hebrew, short spelling (ישוב, הכל, תכנית) and common agreement mistakes, in pages, docs and the Hebrew lines of `.js`, `.mjs` and `.json` (chat lines and guides).
- Static pages: `node scripts/qa-page.cjs <dir> [page-path]`. Needs Playwright: `npm i -D playwright && npx playwright install chromium`.
- Next app: `npm run lint` and `npm run typecheck`. ESLint ignores `demos/**` and `clients/**`.
