# CLAUDE.md

## What this repo is

Two things live here:

1. **Growth OS** (`src/`, `supabase/`): a multi-tenant Next.js app. One deployment serves many business sites, keyed on domain, with AI-proposed copy that the owner approves. See `README.md`.
2. **Kfir's side-income channel** (`demos/`, `clients/`, `docs/side-income/`): lead systems for small local businesses. A landing page, an instant WhatsApp reply, follow-up reminders and a monthly report, sold as a setup fee plus a monthly retainer.

Kfir owns and runs the side-income channel. It is **not** Multibrawn. Never put Multibrawn branding on its pages, and never mix it with other projects.

## House rules for anything a client or prospect sees

- **Hebrew:** correct, short and plain. No spelling mistakes. Address the reader in plural (`אתם`) unless you know who it is.
- **The brand speaks, not a person.** The site, the demos and the AI chat speak as נחיתה רכה, in plural ("אנחנו", "נחזור אליכם"). Never put Kfir's name, or a first-person "I" for the studio, on anything a visitor sees. (A fictional demo business may speak as its own owner.) Outreach messages Kfir sends himself are the exception.
- **No overpromising.** Never promise "double your clients". The promise is "no enquiry gets lost".
- **No yellow** in any palette. Aim for modern and premium, not cheap.
- **Mobile first and RTL.** Every page passes `node scripts/qa-page.cjs <dir> [page]` before it ships.
- **The client owns their assets.** Domain and hosting accounts are registered in the client's name.
- **Paid clients use the official WhatsApp Cloud API.** Unofficial gateways such as Evolution API are for demos and internal use only, because they risk getting the number banned.

## Accessibility menu

Every page (home, `/all/`, `build/`, the panel, all demos, and client sites through the template and `clients/sample-plumber/assets/`) loads `demos/shared/a11y.js` in `<head>`: a round icon at the bottom corner with larger text (four sizes), high contrast, underlined links, a plain font and "עצירת תנועה", kept in localStorage and applied before paint. `data-statement` links the page's accessibility statement. It adds no external library and is not an "overlay" that claims to fix a page: the pages themselves are built to IS 5568 (WCAG AA). Scripts that animate read `html.a11y-still` and listen to the `a11y-still` event (`reel.js`, the home film, the template's video). The publish copies `a11y.js` with `leadbot.js` and `leadform.js`; keep the copy in `clients/sample-plumber/assets/` in sync. The sites use no cookies; keep it that way, because a tracking pixel or analytics would need a consent banner and a new privacy text.

## The lead system (the real backend)

Full guide: `docs/side-income/LEAD_SYSTEM.md`. Netlify Functions on the same site, Supabase for storage (its own project, tables `ls_*` from `supabase/lead-system/0001_lead_system.sql`, not Growth OS), and the official WhatsApp Cloud API. No n8n.

- `POST /api/lead/<slug>` (`lead.mjs`): stores the enquiry, answers the lead from the business's number, alerts the owner with a "טיפלתי" button. Any answer but `{ ok: true }` means the page falls back to opening WhatsApp.
- `/api/wa-webhook`: delivery statuses, the owner's "טיפלתי", replies from leads. Verified with `WA_APP_SECRET`.
- `reminders.mjs` (every 15 minutes), `monthly-report.mjs` (the 1st of the month) and `weekly-report.mjs` (Sunday morning, only where `weekly_report = true`): scheduled.
- Reports: `POST /api/visit/<slug>` (`visit.mjs`, sent once per visit by `LeadForm.visit`) counts visits per day and source in `ls_visits` (`0003_reports.sql`), with no IP, no cookie and nothing identifying. The monthly report, the weekly update and the owner's panel show visits, leads, leads outside working hours, handled, and the top source. Never promise more clients in them; the point is "nothing got lost".
- A photo with the enquiry (`0005_lead_photos.sql`): the chat's photo step (`photo: true` in a `leadbot.js` step) shrinks it in the browser and sends it with the lead. It is stored in the private bucket `lead-photos`, linked in the owner's alert and the panel through `/p/<id>.<signature>` (`lead-photo.mjs`), and deleted with its lead. Without the lead system the demo shows it in the chat and the phone mockup.
- Tagged links (`0006_links.sql`): in the panel the owner names a link per group or channel and gets `<site>/?s=<code>`. `leadform.js` sends it as the source `t-<code>` for the whole visit; the panel, the alert and the monthly report show the name, with visits and leads per link.
- "Available now" (`0007_availability.sql`): the owner writes "עסוק" / "פנוי" / "אוטומטי" to the business's WhatsApp number (or taps it in the panel), and the site's `[data-avail]` line says so, through `GET /api/status/<slug>` (`status.mjs`) and `LeadForm.status()`. It runs out by itself and falls back to the working hours. The plumber demo shows it with "try" buttons (`shared/status.js`).
- When to call back (`0008_callback.sql`): a chat step `callback` ("עכשיו", "בעוד שעה", "היום אחרי 16:00", "היום אחרי 18:00", "מחר בבוקר") becomes `callback_at`; the alert and the panel show it, and the reminder comes 5 minutes before it. A `leadbot.js` step can have `show: (data) => boolean`.
- Review requests (`0009_reviews.sql`), off by default: after "טיפלתי" the owner gets buttons "לשלוח בקשה לביקורת?"; only "כן, לשלוח" sends one `review_request` template, once per number ever, and "הסר" opts the number out. Turned on per business with `review_requests` and `review_url`. Mind the spam law: never send it without the owner's per-customer OK.
- Testimonials on the site (`0010_testimonials.sql`), off by default: after "טיפלתי" the owner can tap "המלצה לאתר"; one `testimonial_request` template sends the customer a private link (`/r/<lead>.<signature>`, `testimonial.mjs`), good once. What they write (plain words: no links, e-mail or phone numbers, up to 400 characters, optional 1 to 5 stars) shows on the site at once through `GET /api/testimonials/<slug>` (`testimonials.mjs`) and `LeadForm.testimonials()` into a `[data-testimonials]` section that stays hidden while empty. The owner gets it on WhatsApp with "להסתיר" and can hide or show any in the panel. Once per number; "הסר" stops it. Never as a rating in Schema.org. The plumber demo shows it with a "נסו" form. Turned on per business with `testimonials`.
- `/panel/` with `/api/leads`: the owner's panel. Owners log in with a key made by `scripts/lead-system/new-client.mjs`; only its hash is stored.
- `POST /api/chat/<slug>` (`client-chat.mjs`): the AI chat on a client's site, from `ls_clients.chat_facts`. It turns a name and a phone into a lead.
- Shared code: `demos/netlify/lib/leads.mjs`. The page side: `demos/shared/leadform.js` and the options of `aibot.js`.
- The home page has no form any more: visitors build in `build/`, and the builder alerts כפיר through `/api/lead/kfir` when a draft is approved.
- Secrets only in Netlify environment variables: `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY`, `WA_TOKEN_<SLUG>`, `WA_APP_SECRET`, `WA_VERIFY_TOKEN`, `LEADS_ADMIN_KEY`, `HEALTH_KEY`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `GITHUB_TOKEN`. Never in the repo or the chat.
- `active = false` on a client stops the system when the monthly payment stops. The page keeps working.
- The page shows "received" only when the lead is stored **and** the owner's WhatsApp alert went out. Otherwise `/api/lead` answers `alert_failed` and the page opens WhatsApp. Failures show in red in the panel and fail `/api/health` (`health.mjs`), which an uptime monitor checks every 5 minutes. Leads older than two years are deleted on the 1st of the month.

## Site builder

Guide: `docs/side-income/SITE_BUILDER.md`. An owner chats at `/build/` (`demos/build/index.html`). Claude fills the fields of a template, and the private draft at `/draft/<id>?k=<key>` (`draft.mjs`) fills up next to the chat. The owner uploads photos and approves, choosing a package. Kfir gets a WhatsApp alert through the lead system (a lead to `kfir`). In `/build/admin/` he marks it paid, returns it with a note, or publishes. Publishing writes `clients/<slug>/` to the repo in one commit through the GitHub API, and for נציג AI also creates the `ls_clients` row.

- Code: `demos/netlify/lib/builder.mjs` and `demos/netlify/functions/builder.mjs` (`/api/builder/:action`), `builder-admin.mjs`, `draft.mjs`.
- Table: `ls_drafts`, plus the `builder` storage bucket, in `supabase/lead-system/0002_site_builder.sql`.
- Templates: `demos/netlify/lib/templates/<id>.mjs`, holding the fields and `render()`. The first one is `plumber.mjs`, built from `clients/sample-plumber/`. Every value is escaped. Facts (years, warranty, prices, response time) come only from the owner.
- The chat uses structured outputs (`output_config.format`), effort low, and fallbacks `"default"`. The history is stored as text only and only grows; the draft state goes into the last message only.
- Extra secrets: `GITHUB_TOKEN`, fine-grained, this repo only, Contents: read and write. Optional: `PAY_URL_*`, `BUILDER_DAILY_MAX`.
- The approval dialog in `demos/build/index.html` shows the prices. Keep it in sync with `#plans`.
- **Design** (`templates/looks.mjs`): three looks on the same content (שרטוט, תעודת אחריות, שלט על הרכב), each with eight palettes, no yellow, all text at WCAG AA contrast. The owner picks one as the questionnaire's first question and sees the draft change; later through "עיצוב: תבנית וצבעים" under the chat, also in self edit. Stored in `content.look` and `content.palette`, sent to `/api/builder/answer` as `style`, no AI. The certificate's seal shows only the owner's own warranty or years.
- **Payment, for now:** no card payment on the site. After approval the owner gets an invoice and payment details on WhatsApp (the invoice comes from a salaried-freelancer company, since כפיר has no עוסק), and כפיר marks it paid in `/build/admin/`.
- **Questionnaire** (`templates/plumber-guide.mjs`, exported as the template's `GUIDE`): one question per topic with answers to pick (regions and their towns, common services with sample price ranges the owner keeps or changes, response time, hours, years, warranty, ready headlines and intros), and a field for what isn't listed. Answers go through `/api/builder/answer` with the chat's own checks and no AI call; the chat stays for anything free-form. A new trade gets its own guide.
- **Self edit** (`0004_self_edit.sql`): after publishing, כפיר can turn on self edit per site in `/build/admin/`. The owner returns to their private `/build/` link and changes the live site in the same chat; nothing goes live until they press "לפרסם את השינויים" (`/api/builder/publish`, one commit to `clients/<slug>/`). They can also discard changes or load the version before the last publish (`live` and `prev_live`). Monthly allowance: `BUILDER_EDIT_MESSAGES` (60) and `BUILDER_EDIT_PUBLISHES` (10). Works only for sites built from a builder template.
- "Save to contacts": a published site gets `contact.vcf` (`vcard()` in the template, served as `text/vcard` by `_headers`) and a link to it. In the panel every tagged link has a QR code to download (SVG or PNG), drawn in the browser with `shared/vendor/qrcode-generator-2.0.4.js` (MIT).
- Guides on client sites: the template shows the trade's urgent guides on the main page (the chat links there) and builds `guides.html` with all of them and FAQPage JSON-LD, from the same `shared/guides/<trade>.json`.
- Schema.org: a published site gets LocalBusiness JSON-LD from the owner's facts (`templates/schema.mjs`: type by trade, phone, towns, hours when they parse, services with price ranges). Nothing invented: no address, no rating. The home page has its own ProfessionalService block.
- Before and after (optional): photo slots `before` and `after` plus `ba_title`. With both photos the site gets a slider (self-contained in the template); a draft shows the frame from the first photo.
- An owner can add one short video (optional). The browser cuts 10 seconds and converts them to an H.264 MP4 with no sound, using Mediabunny (`demos/shared/vendor/`, loaded only when a video is picked). In the template it takes the drawing's place at the top. It plays only on screen, never for reduced motion or save-data, and has a pause button. Tests run the same conversion in VP9, because the test browser has no H.264 encoder.
