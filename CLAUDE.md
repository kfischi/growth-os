# CLAUDE.md

## What this repo is

Two things live here:

1. **Growth OS** (`src/`, `supabase/`): a multi-tenant Next.js app. One deployment serves many business sites, keyed on domain, with AI-proposed copy that the owner approves. See `README.md`.
2. **Kfir's side-income channel** (`demos/`, `clients/`, `docs/side-income/`): lead systems for small local businesses. A landing page, an instant WhatsApp reply, follow-up reminders and a monthly report, sold as a setup fee plus a monthly retainer.

Kfir owns and runs the side-income channel. It is **not** Multibrawn. Never put Multibrawn branding on its pages, and never mix it with other projects.

## House rules for anything a client or prospect sees

- **Hebrew:** correct, short and plain. No spelling mistakes. Address the reader in plural (`אתם`) unless you know who it is.
- **The brand speaks, not a person.** The site, the demos and the AI chat speak as Easy Landing, in plural ("אנחנו", "נחזור אליכם"). Never put Kfir's name, or a first-person "I" for the studio, on anything a visitor sees. (A fictional demo business may speak as its own owner.) Outreach messages Kfir sends himself are the exception.
- **No overpromising.** Never promise "double your clients". The promise is "no enquiry gets lost".
- **No yellow** in any palette. Aim for modern and premium, not cheap.
- **Mobile first and RTL.** Every page passes `node scripts/qa-page.cjs <dir> [page]` before it ships.
- **The client owns their assets.** The domain is registered in the client's name. Builder sites are hosted in כפיר's Netlify account; the site's files are in the repo and are handed over if the client leaves. Pages may say a site is built to the privacy law and the accessibility standard; never promise protection from fines.
- **Paid clients use the official WhatsApp Cloud API.** Unofficial gateways such as Evolution API are for demos and internal use only, because they risk getting the number banned.

## Accessibility menu

Every page (home, `/all/`, `build/`, the panel, all demos, and client sites through the template and `clients/sample-plumber/assets/`) loads `demos/shared/a11y.js` in `<head>`: a round icon at the bottom corner with larger text (four sizes), high contrast, underlined links, a plain font and "עצירת תנועה", kept in localStorage and applied before paint. `data-statement` links the page's accessibility statement. It adds no external library and is not an "overlay" that claims to fix a page: the pages themselves are built to IS 5568 (WCAG AA). Scripts that animate read `html.a11y-still` and listen to the `a11y-still` event (`reel.js`, the home film, the template's video). The publish copies `a11y.js` with `leadbot.js` and `leadform.js`; keep the copy in `clients/sample-plumber/assets/` in sync. The sites use no cookies; keep it that way, because a tracking pixel or analytics would need a consent banner and a new privacy text.

## The lead system (the real backend)

Full guide: `docs/side-income/LEAD_SYSTEM.md`. Netlify Functions on the same site, Supabase for storage (its own project, tables `ls_*` from `supabase/lead-system/0001_lead_system.sql`, not Growth OS), and the official WhatsApp Cloud API. No n8n.

- `POST /api/lead/<slug>` (`lead.mjs`): stores the enquiry, answers the lead from the business's number, alerts the owner with a "טיפלתי" button. Any answer but `{ ok: true }` means the page falls back to opening WhatsApp.
- `/api/wa-webhook`: delivery statuses, the owner's "טיפלתי", replies from leads. Verified with `WA_APP_SECRET`.
- `reminders.mjs` (every 15 minutes), `monthly-report.mjs` (the 1st of the month) and `weekly-report.mjs` (Sunday morning, only where `weekly_report = true`): scheduled.
- Reports: `POST /api/visit/<slug>` (`visit.mjs`, sent once per visit by `LeadForm.visit`) counts visits per day and source in `ls_visits` (`0003_reports.sql`), with no IP, no cookie and nothing identifying. The monthly report, the weekly update and the owner's panel show visits, leads, leads outside working hours, handled, and the top source. Never promise more clients in them; the point is "nothing got lost".
- Statistics (`0013_stats.sql`, `ls_stats`): with each counted visit the device type and the Israel hour; `LeadForm.act()` counts one click per visit on WhatsApp, call, opening the chat (`leadbot.js`, `aibot.js`) and saving the contact, and on the home page "build" and opening a demo (`data-act`), sent to the same `/api/visit/<slug>` as `{ act }`. Numbers per day only, no IP, no cookie (sessionStorage marks only). The panel's "סטטיסטיקות" section shows 30 days from `siteStats()`: visits and leads per day, clicks, devices, busy hours, sources. Keep the privacy texts in step with what is counted.
- A photo with the enquiry (`0005_lead_photos.sql`): the chat's photo step (`photo: true` in a `leadbot.js` step) shrinks it in the browser and sends it with the lead. It is stored in the private bucket `lead-photos`, linked in the owner's alert and the panel through `/p/<id>.<signature>` (`lead-photo.mjs`), and deleted with its lead. Without the lead system the demo shows it in the chat and the phone mockup.
- A first look at the photo (`/api/estimate`, `photo-estimate.mjs`): with `estimate` in `LeadBot.init`, a photo the customer attaches goes to Claude (vision, structured output), which says what it shows and picks one service from the business's own price list; the range is copied from that list, never invented, with "הערכה ראשונית בלבד". Paying sites send their slug and the list comes from `ls_clients.chat_facts` (allowed origins only); the demos (plumber, pest, appliance, garden, ac, locksmith) send their sample `RANGES`, accepted only from our own pages. The photo isn't stored there. Rate limited per IP. Tested in `scripts/tests/photo-estimate.test.mjs`.
- Tagged links (`0006_links.sql`): in the panel the owner names a link per group or channel and gets `<site>/?s=<code>`. `leadform.js` sends it as the source `t-<code>` for the whole visit; the panel, the alert and the monthly report show the name, with visits and leads per link.
- "Available now" (`0007_availability.sql`): the owner writes "עסוק" / "פנוי" / "אוטומטי" to the business's WhatsApp number (or taps it in the panel), and the site's `[data-avail]` line says so, through `GET /api/status/<slug>` (`status.mjs`) and `LeadForm.status()`. It runs out by itself and falls back to the working hours. The plumber demo shows it with "try" buttons (`shared/status.js`).
- Emergency mode (`0014_urgent.sql`): the owner writes "חירום" (6 hours, or "חירום 3") or taps it in the panel; the site's line says "זמינים עכשיו לקריאות דחופות ב<area>" (`ls_clients.area`, set at publish), the chat button reads "קריאה דחופה" (`lf-status` event from `leadform.js` / `status.js`, heard by `leadbot.js`), and the owner alert of a lead that arrives meanwhile starts with "🚨 קריאה בזמן מצב חירום". The plumber demo has a "חירום" try button.
- The photo's first look goes to the owner: `leadbot.js` adds "מהתמונה: …" (what it shows, the matching service and range, urgent or not) to the lead's message and to the WhatsApp text. The alert's details parameter takes up to 480 characters (`sendTemplate` accepts `{ text, max }`).
- The month in ₪ (`0015_job_value.sql`): the owner writes their average job value in the panel; the panel shows the leads marked "נסגר" this month times it, labelled as an estimate, only to them.
- When to call back (`0008_callback.sql`): a chat step `callback` ("עכשיו", "בעוד שעה", "היום אחרי 16:00", "היום אחרי 18:00", "מחר בבוקר") becomes `callback_at`; the alert and the panel show it, and the reminder comes 5 minutes before it. A `leadbot.js` step can have `show: (data) => boolean`.
- Review requests (`0009_reviews.sql`), off by default: after "טיפלתי" the owner gets buttons "לשלוח בקשה לביקורת?"; only "כן, לשלוח" sends one `review_request` template, once per number ever, and "הסר" opts the number out. Turned on per business with `review_requests` and `review_url`. Mind the spam law: never send it without the owner's per-customer OK.

It runs after the webhook answered Meta (`context.waitUntil`). The owner's own messages are never answered by it. Turned on per business with `wa_agent` (נציג AI only).

- **Trades** (`templates/trades/<id>.mjs`, listed in `trades/index.mjs`, the same 25 as the demos): each holds the trade's words (the price list's title, the call to action, "קריאה" or "פנייה"), its three steps, its chat (first question, photo or not, 0–2 extra questions, an urgency question whose first chip is urgent with a tip from its guides), its schema.org type, its guide files (music picks the instrument's guides by the owner's services), its questionnaire answers (services with sample ranges, response times, hours, warranty or none, headlines, intros) and, for the psychologist and the social worker, a crisis line on top and in the chat and a minimal chat. `content.trade_id` picks the trade; `lib/builder.mjs` makes one TEMPLATES entry per trade; the builder's start form has a trade picker (`#fTrade`, prefilled by `?t=<id>` from each demo's "לבנות אתר כזה" link). `scripts/tests/trades.test.mjs` checks every trade's shape and lengths and that its site renders with its own words and parsing scripts; `builder-render.mjs` writes every trade's site and `scripts/tests/trade-chats.cjs` drives each chat to the WhatsApp handoff. The first publish creates the Netlify site with the address `<slug>.<SITES_DOMAIN>` (our own domain on Netlify DNS; the CNAME and the certificate are made by themselves) or `<name>.netlify.app` without it. The addresses go into `allowed_origins`, main one first. A client's own domain: "לחבר דומיין של הלקוח" in `/build/admin/` adds it as an alias and shows the two DNS records; the hourly `checkDomains` in `reminders.mjs` makes it the main address once its DNS points at Netlify (the old address stays and redirects), updates the lead system and alerts כפיר; it gives up after 30 days. `CLIENT.md` is never deployed. Without the token, the manual way in `CLIENT_SITES.md`.

The owner picks one as the questionnaire's first question and sees the draft change; later through "עיצוב: תבנית וצבעים" under the chat, also in self edit. Stored in `content.look` and `content.palette`, sent to `/api/builder/answer` as `style`, no AI. The certificate's seal shows only the owner's own warranty or years.
- **Payment, the address and the automatic publish (Grow)** (`lib/grow.mjs`, `lib/billing.mjs`, `functions/grow-webhook.mjs`, `functions/billing.mjs`, `0017_billing.sql`): after approval the owner picks the address (up to 5 free `<slug>.<SITES_DOMAIN>` names, the business name transliterated by Claude, or their own; optionally their own domain) and pays on Grow's page: monthly (the setup today) or a year ahead (setup plus 10 months). The card is saved as a Grow token. `/api/grow-webhook` never takes the callback at its word: it asks Grow (`getPaymentProcessInfo`), checks the sum, settles once, calls `approveTransaction`, and publishes by itself (`publishDraft`, the same as כפיר's button): repo, hosting, the lead system, and the owner's domain added as waiting. The builder then shows the domain's two DNS records ("כמה שעות, עד 48"), a "לבדוק עכשיו" button, the panel link once, the subscription, invoices and cancel (no notice: it runs to the end of what was paid). `functions/billing.mjs` charges the token daily at the billing day; a refusal retries every 3 days with "לעדכן כרטיס" in the builder, and 4 refusals pause the lead system (the page stays). - **Credit line** at the bottom of every client page: "האתר נבנה ב-<BRAND_NAME> · אתרים שבונים עסקים", linking to `BRAND_URL` (default this site) with `utm_source=client&utm_campaign=<slug>`, the brand's name as the anchor. Env: `BRAND_NAME` (default "Easy Landing"), `BRAND_URL`.

The browser shrinks each (1600 pixels for `hero`, `before` and `after`, 1200 for the rest) and re-encodes it to WebP (JPEG on old Safari, which also drops EXIF); the server takes JPEG or WebP up to 1 MB and stores its width and height (`imageSize`), which the page writes on the `<img>`. A few cents an image; limited per IP and per draft.
- Before and after (optional): photo slots `before` and `after` plus `ba_title`. With both photos the site gets a slider (self-contained in the template); a draft shows the frame from the first photo.
- Videos (optional): one at the top (`video`, `/api/builder/video`) and up to 5 short ones in a "סרטונים מהעבודה" section (`videos`, `/api/builder/clip`, `0019_videos.sql`, each with an optional title up to 34 characters). The top video takes the drawing's place and starts only after the page has loaded. The short videos load nothing with the page: their posters come when the section is a screen away, a file only when it plays (`preload="none"`, the source set then); one plays at a time, only on screen. None plays by itself for reduced motion, save-data, a slow network or `html.a11y-still`; each has a pause button. Measured with Lighthouse 12 (mobile) on the full-media sample (`full.html` from `builder-render.mjs`: a photo at the top, six work photos, before and after, five videos): 207 KB on load against 202 KB for the sample with three work photos. Tests run the same conversion in VP9, because the test browser has no H.264 encoder.
