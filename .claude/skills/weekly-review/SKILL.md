# Weekly review

## 1. Collect (ask only for what's missing)

- Messages sent, replies, demos shown, pilots or clients closed this week
- Active retainers and invoiced amount this month
- Hours spent (the budget is 5 a week)
- Anything blocked (trustee, invoicing company, WhatsApp API approval, a client waiting)

## 2. Update the tracker

- Edit `docs/side-income/TRACKER.md`: flip statuses that changed, add a row to the metrics table, and update the date.
- Don't rewrite history rows. Append only.

## 3. Compare against the goal

The goal is 5,000 ₪ net a month, about 8,500 ₪ invoiced. Show one line:
`ריטיינרים: {n} × {avg} = {mrr} ₪ בחודש · חסר ליעד: {gap} ₪ · בקצב הנוכחי: עוד {months} חודשים`

Use the real numbers only. If the pace is zero, say so plainly.

## 4. Diagnose, honestly

Pick the single biggest bottleneck from the funnel: messages → replies → demos → closes → retention.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Few messages sent | Time and habit | A fixed 20-minute slot, 3 times a week |
| Messages sent, no replies | Opening or targeting | Rewrite the opening with `outreach-message`, switch trade |
| Replies, no demos | Asking too much too early | Offer a sketch, not a meeting |
| Demos, no closes | Price, trust or timing | The pilot offer, a sketch with their name, one follow-up |
| Hours over 5 a week | Building instead of selling | Freeze new building until the next close |

## 5. Output

- The goal line
- The bottleneck, in one sentence
- At most 3 actions for next week, each small enough for one session
- A reminder of any open blocker from stage 0 (the trustee, invoicing, tax coordination) until it is closed

Keep it short and direct. Don't cheerlead, and don't soften a bad week.
