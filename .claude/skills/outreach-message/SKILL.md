# Outreach message

The base templates are in `docs/side-income/OUTREACH.md`. Adapt them to the specific business. Never send a generic template unchanged.

## Inputs

- The business (name, trade, how Kfir knows them: kibbutz member, neighbour, cold)
- The stage: `first` / `follow-up` / `sketch` / `offer` / `reply` (with the prospect's message pasted in)
- The prospect's name, and gender if known. If unknown, write neutrally.

## Rules

- **Kfir's voice:** first person, masculine (`אני בונה`, `מחפש`), signed "כפיר". Never Multibrawn.
- **Length:** up to 4–5 short lines. One question or one call to action, never two.
- **Opening:** start with their world (a question about lost enquiries, slow replies, or something specific to their trade), not with what Kfir sells.
- **Promise:** "אף פנייה לא הולכת לאיבוד". No numbers, no "guaranteed", no "double".
- **Price:** don't put the price in a first message. In `offer`, state it exactly as in `CLAUDE.md`, and mention the three-payment setup.
- **Follow-up:** at most one, 3–4 days later. After that, stop.
- **Replying to an objection** (`reply` stage): acknowledge it in one line, answer it in one line, and offer the smallest next step (a 5-minute demo, a sketch).

## Output

1. The message, ready to paste.
2. One line on why this angle was chosen.
3. Optionally, one alternative opening line.

## Trade angles (examples)

| Trade | Angle |
| --- | --- |
| Technicians, plumbers, electricians | Calls missed while on the job |
| Therapists, cosmetics | Booking enquiries during a session |
| Holiday units, events venues | Price questions at night, guests who book elsewhere |
| Tutors, coaches | Parents asking the same questions again and again |
