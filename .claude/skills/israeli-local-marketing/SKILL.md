# Marketing local services in Israel

Write all copy with the `hebrew-copy` skill. House rules apply: no overpromising, the client owns their accounts, and nothing fake.

## 1. Channels, in order of payoff for a local trade

1. **Google Business Profile (גוגל מפות).** Searches like "אינסטלטור בחדרה" mostly end in the map results.
   - Fill in hours, service area and the WhatsApp link.
   - Add real photos every week.
   - Answer every review by name, within a day.
2. **WhatsApp.**
   - Status: photos from jobs, two or three a week.
   - A broadcast list (רשימת תפוצה) of past customers who agreed to receive messages: a seasonal reminder once a month at most.
   - Not groups and not mass messaging.
3. **Local Facebook groups** (the town or neighbourhood group, the local board).
   - Questions like "מישהו מכיר חשמלאי טוב?" are a real source of leads. The owner answers personally and briefly.
   - Read the group rules first. Many groups allow ads only on certain days.
4. **Referrals and word of mouth.**
   - After a good job, ask for a recommendation.
   - Send a ready-made message the customer can forward.
5. **Trade directories** (Midrag, Easy, B144). Reviews on Midrag carry real weight for trades.
6. **Instagram and TikTok.** Worth it mainly for visual trades: renovation, cleaning (before and after), trainers. Low priority for urgent trades.
7. **Paid ads, only after the basics are in place.**
   - Google Ads on local searches for urgent trades, with a small daily budget.
   - Facebook and Instagram for visual trades.
   - The budget is set together with the client and paid from the client's own account.

## 2. Seasons in Israel (rules of thumb; check them against the client's data)

| Trade | Peak | What to do before it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| מיזוג אוויר | Servicing in April and May, breakdowns in July and August, switching to heating in October | A cleaning reminder in April, with a real deadline |
| ניקיון | Before Pesach (March to April), before Rosh Hashana, after renovations | "ניקיון לפסח" from February, with limited slots only if that's true |
| אינסטלציה | Winter (water heaters, blockages in the rain), the kitchen before Pesach | Water-heater content in November |
| חשמל | Winter (heaters, storms), EV chargers all year | Safety content before the first storm |
| הובלות | Summer, before the school year, and the end of each month (leases start on the 1st) | Booking early from May |
| שיפוצים | People plan in winter and want it done before the holidays | Show timelines in winter |
| כושר | After the autumn holidays ("אחרי החגים"), January, before summer | An intro-session campaign at the end of September |

**Timing:**
- Sunday to Thursday, 7:00 to 9:00 and 20:00 to 22:00.
- Friday morning works well in local groups.
- No posts on Friday afternoon or Shabbat, and none on Yom Kippur.
- No "urgent" promotions on holidays or days of mourning.

## 3. Offers that don't feel like a sales pitch

- **Lower the risk:**
  - "מחיר סגור לפני שמתחילים"
  - "אחריות 12 חודשים"
  - "בדיקה בלי עלות" (only if it really is free)
- **A real deadline:** "ניקוי מזגן ב-220 ₪ עד סוף מאי". No made-up scarcity ("נשארו רק 2 מקומות!!!").
- **A referral offer:** "המלצתם על שכן? 50 ₪ הנחה לשניכם."

## 4. Ready templates (adapt every one; never send as is)

**Answering in a local group** (from the owner, not a fake customer):
> היי, אורן מחדרה, אינסטלטור כבר 12 שנה. אפשר לכתוב לי בוואטסאפ ל-052-0000000 ולשלוח תמונה של התקלה. אם זה דחוף, אני חוזר מהר.

**Asking for a Google review after a job** (WhatsApp, the same day):
> היי דנה, תודה על היום. אם יצאת מרוצה, ביקורת קצרה בגוגל תעזור לי מאוד: [קישור]. דקה, לא יותר. אורן

**Replying to a good review:**
> תודה, משה! שמחתי לעזור עם הדוד. אם משהו צריך בדיקה, אני כאן.

**Replying to a negative review:** calm and specific, and move the conversation to private:
> היי רונית, מצטער שזה הרגיש ככה. ראיתי את הקריאה מה-12 במרץ ואשמח לחזור ולבדוק את החיבור. אפשר לכתוב לי ישירות ל-052-0000000.

**Seasonal WhatsApp status:**
> החום מגיע. יש תורים לניקוי מזגן בשבוע הבא, בחדרה והסביבה. לקבוע? כתבו "מזגן".

**Before-and-after post** (cleaning or renovation):
> לפני ואחרי מהבוקר בפרדס חנה. ארבעה חדרים, חמש שעות, ומיקרוגל שלא ראה מטלית מאז החורף. רוצים הערכה לבית שלכם? הקישור בתגובה הראשונה.

(In Israeli Facebook groups the link usually goes in the first comment.)

**Google Ads** (count the characters):
- Headlines (up to 30): "אינסטלטור בחדרה" · "מחיר סגור לפני העבודה" · "וואטסאפ ותמונה של התקלה"
- Description (up to 90): "סתימות, נזילות ודודים בחדרה והסביבה. שולחים תמונה בוואטסאפ ומקבלים טווח מחיר."

## 5. Measuring

- Tag every source:
  - Add a parameter to each link (`?src=fb-hadera`, `?src=gmb`, `?src=status`).
  - Or have the chatbot ask "איך הגעתם אלינו?" as its last question.
- The `monthly-report` skill groups leads by source. A channel that brought nothing in two months gets less time.

## 6. What we never do

- **No fake reviews.** Don't write reviews for the client, don't publish "recommendations" from fake profiles, and don't buy followers.
- **No mass WhatsApp, SMS or email ads to people who didn't agree.** The spam law (חוק הספאם, section 30A of the Communications Law) requires prior consent for advertising messages. The broadcast list is only for customers who said yes.
  - A first message to a business should be personal and ask whether they're interested (see `outreach-message`).
  - If a case is unclear, check with someone who knows the law. Don't guess.
- **No spam in groups**, and no copying the same post into twenty groups.
- **No promises** of more clients, "double the income" or guaranteed results.
